
# Accessing Other American Science Cloud Resoures

If you are associated with Fermilab, and want to be able to log into
American Science Cloud and use its resources, you can do so with your
Fermilab Single Sign On (SSO) credentials, but only once you have requested
and been granted access.   To make such a request, you currently need to
send an email to: `SCD-AmSC-Federated-ID@listserv.fnal.gov` giving your name, 
and a project or list of projects with which you are planning to use 
American Science Cloud.

Once your request has been approved and completed, you will recieve a 
confirmation email, and you will be able to access resources like the
[AmSC MAG Interface](https://mag.genesis.americansciencecloud.org) 
with your Fermilab credentials.
When you go to that, or other American Science Cloud webpages, you will
get a login page that lets you choose your identity provider.  You should
choose "Fermilab (FNAL)", and press "Continue", then when you get to the 
Fermilab single sign-on page, choose which sort of two-factor login you 
want to use, and log in with your Fermilab SSO / Services password
and selected second authentication factor.
